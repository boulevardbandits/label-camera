Label Camera

Type the lot number, then point at a GM, ACDelco, Ford, Motorcraft or Mopar label. It grabs the barcode right away, reads the part number from the label, and double-beeps when it's sure. If it won't read live, tap the big white Capture button: it takes a sharp full photo and reads that in the background while you keep going. Tap Send to scanner, then 📥 in the Iowa scanner.
